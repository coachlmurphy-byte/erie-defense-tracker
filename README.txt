Erie Defensive Tracker V0.9c

V0.9c is based on the working V0.9b build.

CSV export improvements:
- Every play row now includes Game Date, Opponent, and Location.
- CSV filename includes opponent and game date when available.
- Defensive call fields remain separate columns.

Use V0.9c for testing. It preserves the working V0.9b call selection and yardage workflow.


V0.9d: Added TD and FG to Play Result. Both end the defensive series and reset to 1st & 10 for the next possession.
